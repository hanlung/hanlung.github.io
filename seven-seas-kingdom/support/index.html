Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Seven Seas Kingdom runs entirely on your device and makes no network requests of its own. Your fleet keeps working while the app is closed — progress is caught up from the clock when you return, not from a server.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to another device?

Your save is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is enabled for the game, it can also sync through your own iCloud account so you can continue on another device. There are no accounts or logins in the game itself.

How do I start over?

Settings → Start New Campaign erases your local progress and begins a fresh game. Deleting the app also removes local save data.

Can I change the language?

Yes. The game ships in English, 繁體中文, and 日本語, and starts in your device's language by default. You can pick a different one any time in Settings → Language; it applies immediately.

The text is too small.

Settings has a text-size selector. Every screen re-lays out to the size you choose.

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. This version is free, contains no advertising, and has no in-app purchases or payment code of any kind.

Privacy
