Privacy Policy — Seven Seas Kingdom

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Seven Seas Kingdom ("the Game", "we", "us") is published by Uniwind. This policy explains what information the Game does and does not handle. The short version: the Game does not collect, store, or share personal data on our own servers — we don't run any.

Information we collect

None that identifies you. Seven Seas Kingdom has no user accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not collect your name, email, location, device identifiers, contacts, or usage analytics.

Game saves

Your progress is stored locally on your device. Optionally, it may also be synced through your own Apple iCloud account (iCloud Key-Value storage), if you are signed in to iCloud and iCloud is enabled for the Game, so you can continue on your other devices.

In that case the save lives in your own Apple account, under your control — we cannot access it, because we operate no servers of our own. The save contains only game progress (ships, crew, resources, islands); it contains no personal information. You can erase your local progress at any time from Settings → Start New Campaign, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

This version of the Game is free and contains no in-app purchases. It ships without any payment or purchasing code at all, so there is nothing for us or anyone else to process. Should a future version add optional purchases, they would be handled entirely by the App Store, we would never see or store your payment details, and this policy would be updated first.

Notifications

The Game may schedule local notifications on your device (for example, when a voyage arrives). These are generated and delivered on your device; no information is sent to us or to any server to deliver them.

Feedback you send us

If you use Menu → Send Feedback, your device opens its email app with a message addressed to us. If you choose to send it, we receive the email address you send from and the contents of your message. We use this only to read and respond to your feedback. Sending feedback is entirely optional.

Third-party services

The Game relies only on the platform services of the store it was installed from:

  • Apple (App Store, iCloud) — https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Apple's handling of any data is governed by its own policy. The Game contains no advertising, analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own. It is built with the open-source Godot Engine, which does not collect player data.

Children's privacy

Seven Seas Kingdom is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The Game contains no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, no advertising, and no in-app purchases.

Data retention and your choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our side. Your save data is removed when you start a new campaign or uninstall the Game. Any cloud copy is managed by you through Apple ID / iCloud settings. The Game has no accounts of its own, so there is no account for us to delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
